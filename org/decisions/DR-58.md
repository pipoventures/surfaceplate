## Decision

1. **The coverage definition: tiered, every reachable decision.** The validation runs the wizard
   in full for every conformance level × interface answer × repository shape (bare, rich,
   mixed); exercises every gate in every status in every configuration; every artefact field
   with a found file, the seed row and a typed path; every above-floor control on and off; every
   route - the interactive flow, `--propose` then `--answers`, resume after every stage, `--edit`
   on every editable leaf class, cancel at every point, and every refusal class - and drives the
   screens by keys through a full run at every configuration. Cross-gate status interactions are
   argued independent from the code (`sections.build_gate` builds each gate from its own answers
   alone) and stated as such; the literal sweep of every status combination (3^11 at `standard`)
   is named and not done. Free text is one equivalence class per field, a distinct sentinel per
   run. The `advanced` explanation register changes text only and is excluded from the tiers.
2. **The record: a generated report and a signed record.** The suite writes a deterministic report
   under `audit/validation/`, tracked, with a `--check` mode that fails CI when it is stale; and
   one assurance-evidence record under `governance/assurance/`, validating against the standard's
   own `assurance-evidence.schema.yaml`. The agent runs the validation and drafts the record with
   the measured counts and the evidence gaps as limitations; the record's `outcome` and
   `reviewer_identity` are the maintainer's, set by merging. An agent may not conclude a
   validation.
3. **Where it runs: every CI run, as the sixteenth step**, with its outcome confirmed by the
   every-step-ran loop like every other.

## Context and alternatives

Alternatives put and not chosen: the tiers plus an offline exhaustive sweep where finite and a
recorded random sample where not (rejected: no oracle stronger than the tiered one); the
literal sweep (infeasible: 177,147 runs at `standard` alone); a report without a signed record
(nothing would state who concluded what); no document at all, the suite as the evidence
(nothing to point a complainant at beyond a green step); an on-demand job instead of a CI step
(the record could drift from the code between runs).

## Impact

- Numerical/model output: none. Contracts/schemas: none; the record instantiates an existing
  schema. Security/data: none.
- Reproducibility/lineage: the report carries no dates or temporary paths, so two runs on two
  machines produce the same bytes; the date lives in the record.
- Operations/release: one more suite, minutes long; `CLAUDE.md` says fourteen.

## Evidence

## Limitations and follow-up

The evidence gaps stated in advance and carried into the record: no real terminal; one Python
and one Textual on Linux; free text as one class per field; cross-gate interactions argued, not
swept; the history audit exercised only against fresh fixture history; the checker assumed
correct, since it is the oracle.
