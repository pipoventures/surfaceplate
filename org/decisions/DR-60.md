## Decision

**The rule stays as it is.** A later instant on the same calendar day as an earlier declared date
is a forward move of `effective_from`, and `SP034` blocks it, ungraced. The message prints each
value as declared (`ACT-054`), so the reader sees an instant against a date rather than the same
date twice.

## Context and alternatives

`F92`: a scratch re-adoption on the day of an earlier adoption declared a gate effective from the
instant the scaffold created its artefact, hours after the midnight the earlier profile's bare date
means; the audit window narrowed by those hours and the checker blocked. The rule was right by its
letter; the question was whether the letter should tolerate a same-day move.

Not chosen: tolerating a move within the same calendar day (a documented hole of up to a day in
the audit window, on the field that decides how much history a gate covers); having the wizard
read the profile's history and propose the earlier value (a new behaviour for a case seen once,
on a scratch copy). The cost of the rule as it stands falls on a same-day re-adoption, which types
the earlier value back by hand; the message now makes that obvious.
