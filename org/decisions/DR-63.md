## Decision

1. **`effective_from`'s fraction follows seconds or is absent.** The pattern admitted
   `14:30.500`; it now admits a fraction only after seconds, in the schema and in the shared
   `rules` module the checker and the wizard read. Calendar validity stays the checker's
   (`SP033`): a pattern cannot know February's length, and the checker and the field already
   refuse an impossible date.
2. **`adoption_status: complete` requires `status_rationale`**, as `blocked` and `deferred`
   did. No evidence reference is required there: lifecycle, validation and approval are kept in
   separate places on purpose (`core/CONTROL_PRINCIPLES.md` principle 7), and evidence lives in
   assurance-evidence records under `governance/`. The wizard asks the rationale for `complete`
   as it does for the other two.

## Context and alternatives

Not chosen: leaving the pattern as documentation of a form the checker does not enforce; a
calendar-validating pattern (impossible in a regex); requiring an evidence reference on
`complete` (folds two states into one field); leaving `complete` unexplained.

## Impact

- Contracts/schemas: the profile schema narrows in two places; the schema contract version is
  unchanged, since neither form removed could appear in a valid profile written by this framework.
- Adopters: a profile declaring `complete` without a rationale fails validation at its next check
  and says which line; this repository's own profile already carries one.
- Documentation: the template's comment; this record.

## Evidence

`tests/validate_contracts.py`: eleven cases, seen to fail first on the unexplained `complete`.
