## Decision

1. When Ctrl+S is pressed on the gate list while gates beyond the floor are folded and undecided,
   the screen does not refuse by naming a gate the reader cannot see. It asks one question,
   naming the level and the count: declare them all not applicable, recorded as one human act
   with its count, or open them and decide each. Quitting the question changes nothing.
2. The rule of `DR-47` (4) stands: the tool never supplies a scope decision. The question is the
   bulk command, put where the reader meets the need for it rather than left in the key legend.
3. With the fold open, an undecided gate still refuses Ctrl+S by name, as before, because the
   reader can see it.

## Context and alternatives

Continuing silently, with the folded gates declared not applicable by the tool, was rejected:
that is the tool deciding scope, which `F62` recorded the cost of. Keeping the refusal and
improving its wording was rejected: it still names something folded away.
