## Decision

1. Fields the checker never reads become optional in `application-profile.schema.yaml` and are
   stamped `# not checked` by the renderer when present: `display_name`,
   `repository_classification`, `human_roles`, `exclusions`, and `independent_validator` at
   `essential`. The two gate description fields stay, because humans and auditors read them.
   Both shipped examples and the template are updated in the same change; existing profiles
   remain valid because nothing is removed.
2. Two optional fields are added under `risk`: `relied_on_outside_team` and
   `material_quantitative_output`, so the reason a level was chosen is recorded rather than
   asked and discarded.
3. The wizard's draft moves from `.surfaceplate-adopt-draft.json` at the repository root to
   `.standards/adopt-draft.json`, which the install record already governs; the installer does
   not edit an adopter-owned `.gitignore`. A draft at the old location is offered once and then
   migrated.
4. `pytest==8.4.2`, `pytest-textual-snapshot==1.1.0` and `syrupy==4.8.0` are added as a `test`
   extra in `pyproject.toml`. Snapshots under `tests/__snapshots__/` are golden files under the
   installed tests rule: a deliberate change carries cause, delta and reviewer, and
   `--snapshot-update` is never used to absorb an unexplained difference. The twelve existing
   suites keep their runner and their count-on-success; pytest runs the snapshot suite only, and
   the workflow's "every step ran" loop and `CLAUDE.md`'s count gain one row.
5. Docstrings describe current behaviour in a sentence and point at the decision record for
   history. Existing essays move to `org/decisions/` or the changelog as the code beneath them
   is touched, not in one sweep.

## Context and alternatives

The review found the checker never reads twelve profile fields the wizard asks for, that the
two reliance answers are asked and discarded, that the draft sits untracked in the adopter's
tree with nothing ignoring it, that no suite renders a gate's status row, and that docstrings
had been wrong about their code (`F55`, and twelve more discrepancies in the review's §7).
The spike proved snapshot testing works against textual 8.2.8 in 0.22 s and is deterministic.

Alternatives considered: drop the never-read fields (rejected: breaks every existing profile
and both examples); gitignore the draft from the installer (rejected: the installer writing
outside `.standards/`); keep the hand-rolled render properties only (kept as well; snapshots are
added, not substituted); move all docstring history at once (rejected: a sweep with no test).
