## Decision

A control the checker verifies through a prerequisite gate is offered above the floor only at a
level that declares that gate. Today that is one pair: `documentation_authority`, verified through
`authority_map`, is withheld from the above-floor list at `essential`, and the list's help says
why, naming the gate and `SP052`. The pairing lives in `plan.WITHHELD_ABOVE_FLOOR` so a second
such control joins by one line, not a second rule.

## Context and alternatives

The matrix (`ACT-057`, tier T4) ticked every above-floor control at every level. At `essential`
with `documentation_authority` ticked the wizard wrote a profile the checker faulted at once:
`SP052` closes the seam `core/CONFORMANCE_LEVELS.md` names - a level is a floor, an adopter may
require the control at `essential`, and it would then be checked against nothing because the
`authority_map` gate is not part of that floor. The wizard knew the checker's rule and offered the
combination anyway.

Alternatives not chosen: (a) declare the `authority_map` gate when the control is ticked - the
gate list precedes the remainder form, so the gate's artefact, paths and date would have to be
asked on a later screen or the flow reordered, for one control at one level; (b) leave the
combination offered and let the first check report it - the wizard would be writing a profile it
knows the checker faults, which is the class of answer `DR-48` made it refuse at the field. An
adopter at `essential` who wants the control chooses `standard`, where both are the floor, or
edits the profile by hand to add the gate; the help says so.
